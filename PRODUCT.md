# Product: Observation Bench — Science Lab

## Product surface

Observation Bench is a small experiment notebook for changing one variable, reading a synthetic curve, and recording an observation.

## Primary user

A student or curious builder learning to frame an experiment before reaching for a complete lab platform.

## Product promise

Make the variable visible so the observation can be discussed.

## Boundaries

- All measurements are synthetic.
- Do not imply laboratory validation or safety guidance.
- The demo focuses on the observation loop, not a complete curriculum.

## Design mode

operate

## Interaction contract

- Experiment tabs change the variable label and curve.
- The slider changes the selected value and readout.
- Record observation adds a local note to the bench log.
